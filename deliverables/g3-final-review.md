## Material findings and dispositions

### G3-01 — Medium: missing verification dates were represented as contradictory facts

The original inline date comparison in `credentialing/evidence.py::evaluate_replies` compared blank verified dates against declared dates. A no-record reply therefore manufactured a date discrepancy instead of retaining unknown dates and outstanding verification. Actual APP-2026-021 / ENT-2100 is affected in B2/B3. B05 and B36 require preserving actual competing values and distinguishing unknowns; B07 still requires an owned impossibility/secondary-route determination. The correction must remove only the false contradiction, preserve the no-record response and closure evidence, and leave verification/accreditation unresolved. B1 has no such changed outcome.

Disposition: corrected and checked. `_date_conflicts` now requires an actual competing value; a separate missing-boundary check prevents absent dates from verifying an interval. Gap reconciliation uses the structured discrepancy flag. `tests/test_no_record.py` has seven focused checks covering actual APP-021 across all exports, missing boundaries, historical cutoff, real contradictions and revision controls. The reviewed staged B2/B3 outcomes remove the false gap hold while education/accreditation remain outstanding.

### G3-02 — Medium: named satisfaction could not close a monitored condition

`credentialing/engine.py::project` copied all admitted approval conditions into every later projection and called each one unmet when its due date arrived. It never consumed a record naming a satisfied condition. This contradicts `sources/procedure.md` §10 (line 213), interview E-3 20:48 and B33, and misses the implementation plan's explicit named-satisfaction check. Existing `tests/test_activation.py` covered due/unmet conditions only. A supplied valid satisfaction record would therefore continue to generate a false Clinical Director action.

Required bounded correction: match explicit recorded satisfaction to the exact application/revision/approval/condition, preserve its provenance, and stop tracking only that satisfied condition. Unnamed, future, mismatched or unsupported records must not silently clear obligations. A supported parser representation must be described as an engineering format, not invented office policy or a new exclusive authority rule.

Disposition: corrected and checked. `credentialing/conditions.py::condition_satisfaction` validates exact application/revision/approval/condition/due-date bindings, receipt/export dates, explicit outcome and rostered signed author. `engine.project` removes only satisfied running conditions and retains records in assessment history; rejected records create MSP review work. Six checks in `tests/test_condition_satisfaction.py` cover valid Clinical Director records, routine MSP re-verification, mismatches/unknown/future/unsigned evidence, and connected due-action closure. Limited supported author/formats are explicitly parser limitations, not new office authority rules. The three actual exports have no admitted approval, so positive satisfaction evidence remains synthetic.

### G3-03 — Medium: a surplus unanswered peer still blocked a complete peer requirement

`credentialing/evidence.py::evaluate_replies` emitted a blocking `references/outstanding` issue for each unanswered referee. Obtaining two usable peers only suppressed the aggregate minimum-count issue; the earlier surplus issue still made `engine.presentable` false. `sources/procedure.md` §3 Check 4 and B10 explicitly permit presentation once two eligible current peers are established, retaining extra open requests until actual presentation. The planned surplus-peer boundary was absent from existing tests.

Required bounded correction: retain the open request, attempts and owned work without blocking a met peer requirement; close it only on documented presentation, never as a satisfied or counted reply. Losing one qualifying peer through staleness must restore the requirement hold.

Disposition: corrected and checked. Surplus unanswered requests become retained `pending-work` once two distinct usable peers exist. This internal tag neither changes the schema nor counts silence as a reply. `element_states`/`presentable` ignore that nonblocking work; an admitted same-revision decision supplies recorded presentation for closure, whose reason/reference remains in assessment history. `tests/test_evidence_cures.py` checks two-peer, one-peer, duplicate-peer and absent-provenance cases; `tests/test_connected_workflow.py` checks readiness and presentation closure through the processor. Actual supplied peers still lack direct-return provenance, so eligible CSV replies alone cannot establish the positive case.

### G3-04 — Medium: a rejected reply remained active work after usable replacement evidence arrived

Both `credentialing/evidence.py::evaluate_replies` and `credentialing/privileges.py::evaluate_privileges` created current outstanding issues while iterating rejected replies, then retained those issues even when later replies established usable verification. This conflated permanent evidence history with present unresolved work. B06/B08/B23 require retaining rejected material and attempts while allowing admissible evidence to establish verification. Actual Batch-3 APP-2026-020 / ENT-2000 accepts VR-2003 but still carries a current source-rejection issue for earlier applicant-routed VR-2000. Missing accreditation remains a legitimate separate hold.

Required bounded correction: retain rejected rows/reasons in history and entry checks; clear only obsolete missing/provenance work resolved by usable evidence. Preserve actual contradictory facts, unsupported revised facts, and independently missing accreditation. Include invalid-then-valid and genuine-conflict controls for the affected evidence paths.

Disposition: corrected and checked. Resolved missing/source-rejection work is retained as nonblocking `pending-work` with both rejected and qualifying evidence references. Entry rows, reasons and attempts remain; actual date/revised-fact conflicts remain blocking, as do certification board mismatches and same-day contradictory facts. `tests/test_evidence_cures.py` provides nine focused checks including receipt order and historical cutoff. Staged real B3 changes only APP-020's action wording for this correction; its missing accreditation and other holds remain.

### G3-05 — High: mentioning a practice-question code could stand in for a human disposition

`credentialing/dispositions.py::clinical_dispositions` extracted `ppq_codes` from every PPQ token in an authorized document's title/text. `credentialing/engine.py::assess` then attached disposition evidence to the matching high-risk finding without checking what disposition had actually been recorded. An otherwise correctly signed record saying PPQ-2 remains under review and directing that the file not be presented would therefore remove that finding's readiness hold. Authorship is not the reserved human judgment required by B22/B25 and P §§4,8.

Required bounded correction: distinguish mere mention from an explicit supported affirmative disposition; retain negative, pending and unrecognized wording as unresolved. Add a control proving that a rostered signature plus a PPQ code alone cannot clear the wait. Actual APP-032's April 2 record expressly directs presentation with evidence and records no bar/condition, so no changed real outcome is asserted.

Disposition: corrected and checked. `clinical_dispositions` now records a separate `ppq_disposition_recorded` flag only for the supported complete affirmative presentation sentence, rejecting conditional/pending/negative wording. `assess` requires that flag before ending the high-risk wait. `tests/test_connected_workflow.py::test_high_risk_mention_does_not_substitute_for_returned_disposition` exercises mere mention, pending/negative text, conditional presentation (`after`/`if`) and a genuine affirmative disposition through computed readiness. The original finding remains in the packet after disposition.
